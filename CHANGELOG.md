## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-02

### Upgrade and limits

- Initial pre-1.0 release. The independent security review, support commitments and other 1.0 readiness gates remain open; this version does not assert that they are complete.
- Back up the durable store and retained workspace state before replacing binaries. Keep existing policies and credential boundaries when upgrading.
- Live close and merge remain unavailable. Comments require the explicit comment policy, and automatic documentation repair remains off until its policy is enabled.

### Added

- Policy-governed environments, durable sessions, turns, runners, events and actions.
- Immutable review records and dry-run apply for close, with scoped runner and guest boundaries.
- Governed write proposals in implement sessions, separate from review sessions and guest execution.
- Operator diagnostics, audit and recovery tools, and versioned server and runner artifacts.
